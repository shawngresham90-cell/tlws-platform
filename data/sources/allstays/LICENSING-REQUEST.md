**Subject:** Licensing a structured truck-stop and parking feed

Hello,

I run Trucking Life With Shawn — a driver-facing site with a truck-stop, rest
area and truck-parking directory, plus a trip planner.

I've been building the directory from authoritative sources only: official
operator exports for the chains, and state DOT and FHWA data for public
facilities. That approach works for Love's, Pilot/Flying J and TA. It falls
apart for **independent truck stops**, because no one publishes a national list
of them. You do, and I'd rather license it than approximate it.

I'm writing to ask about a **structured data feed under license**, not to scrape
anything. To be explicit: I have not scraped your site, I'm not using your app,
and I haven't bought the PDF to extract from.

What I'd want covered:

- **Independent truck stops** — the category I can't source anywhere else
- Rest areas and welcome centers
- Weigh and inspection stations, as a **separate** category from parking
- Truck parking locations generally

Per record, the fields that matter to a driver:

- a stable location ID
- name, address, city, state, ZIP
- **latitude and longitude**
- **direction of travel** and exit or milepost — a northbound facility is not a
  southbound one, and treating them as one is a real safety problem
- truck parking spaces, where known
- access hours and any duration limit
- amenities
- status, so closures are detectable

And commercially:

- **redistribution rights** for display on our site
- **update rights** — a refresh on a schedule, not a one-time dump
- attribution terms, which I'm glad to meet prominently
- pricing for a small independent publisher

I'm a one-person operation, not a funded aggregator, so I'd want to start with
whatever tier makes sense and grow into it. If a revenue-share or affiliate
arrangement fits better than a flat license, I'm open to that too.

If you'd rather not license the data, I'd still appreciate knowing — I'll plan
around the gap rather than work around you.

Thanks,
Shawn Gresham
Trucking Life With Shawn
truckinglifewithshawn.com

## Notes for the conversation

**What makes this worth their while.** Directional data and independent-stop
coverage are their differentiators. A licensee that attributes them on every
listing and refreshes on schedule is a distribution channel, not a competitor.

**What we will not ask for.** Their exclusive locations as a bulk dump with no
attribution, or any right to sublicense.

**The honest fallback if they decline.** Independent truck stops stay
uncovered. They are **not** part of gate lines 1–6 as written, so the launch
gate can still pass without them — coverage is measured per named source, and no
gate counts a row from an unlicensed source. It would be a product gap, not a
launch blocker.
