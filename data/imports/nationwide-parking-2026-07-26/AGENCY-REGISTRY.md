# Official agency acquisition registry — gate lines 5 and 6

Generated by `scripts/generate-agency-registry.mjs`. Do not hand-edit;
change the generator so the ranking stays reproducible.

**Every agency host is blocked at this environment’s egress proxy** 
(`connect_rejected`, 403 CONNECT). No URL here was fetched or verified.
Entry points are the canonical place to start and must be confirmed on
arrival. Nothing here asserts that a dataset exists at a given path today.

---

## Why this registry is the launch’s critical path

Operator exports are obtainable — Love’s and Pilot both landed. Agency data
is not, and gate line 5 needs **≥ 95 % of official public rest areas, welcome
centers and service plazas**. Line 5 will still be open long after lines 2–4
close, so it sets the launch date.

## Ranking basis

Rank is **computed**, not asserted: each state scores the sum over the
priority corridors it carries, weighted inversely by the parking the
directory already publishes on that corridor.

| Corridor | Mappable published parking today | Weight |
|---|--:|--:|
| **I-95** | 4 | 12.5 |
| **I-80** | 28 | 3.1 |
| **I-90/I-94** | 27 | 3.2 |
| **I-10** | 23 | 3.7 |
| **I-15** | 7 | 9.1 |

**I-95 dominates the ranking** because the directory publishes just **4**
mappable parking rows on it nationwide. A state that carries I-95 is worth
more than one that carries I-80, which already has 28.

## Ask the federal layer first

**FHWA / BTS national truck parking layer (Jason’s Law survey)** — Federal Highway Administration / Bureau of Transportation Statistics

One request covers all 50 states at once. Lower resolution and possibly stale, but it is the only single artifact that can move gate line 5 nationally in one step.

> **Conflict rule:** State DOT data WINS over FHWA on any conflict — the state is closer to the asset. Record the federal layer vintage and never let it override a fresher state dataset.

## Toll and turnpike authorities are separate custodians

A state DOT rest-area dataset **does not** contain turnpike service plazas.
On the northeast I-95 / I-80 / I-90 corridors that is most of the truck-usable
stopping capacity, so missing them leaves a hole exactly where coverage is
worst.

## Weigh stations are a separate request, and a separate category

Enforcement data usually sits with the **state police or highway patrol**,
not the DOT. Asking the DOT for scale locations wastes a cycle.

> A weigh, inspection, scale, check station or port of entry is **never**
> counted as truck parking unless the authoritative source explicitly
> confirms legal parking is permitted.

They are captured at 100 % as their **own category** so a driver can see and
avoid them.

## Fields every dataset must carry

A checklist to verify on arrival — **not** a schema to code against before
the file exists.

- `facility_id (stable, namespaced by state)`
- `name`
- `facility_type (rest area | welcome center | service plaza | weigh station)`
- `route`
- `direction`
- `milepost_or_exit`
- `latitude`
- `longitude`
- `truck_spaces`
- `truck_parking_permitted (explicit boolean — REQUIRED for weigh stations)`
- `hours_or_seasonal_restrictions`
- `status (open | closed | seasonal | under construction)`

`truck_parking_permitted` is **required** for any weigh or inspection
station. Without it the row is captured as a weigh station and never as
parking.

## Rules that bind every one of these intakes

- **weigh station is not parking** — A weigh, inspection, scale, check station or port of entry is never counted as truck parking unless the authoritative source explicitly confirms legal parking is permitted.
- **no inferred coordinates** — No coordinate from a road midpoint, ZIP centroid, interpolation or geocoder guess.
- **no invented attributes** — Space count, hours, restrictions and amenities stay null unless the source states them.
- **directional pairs stay separate** — A northbound and a southbound facility are two facilities and need two coordinates.
- **toll authorities are separate custodians** — A state DOT rest-area dataset does not contain turnpike or thruway service plazas. Those must be requested from the authority that operates them.
