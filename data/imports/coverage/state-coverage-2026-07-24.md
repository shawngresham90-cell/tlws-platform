- Pennsylvania (PA) is **uncovered** and is the Milestone 6 target (I-76/
  I-80/I-81 + connectors), followed by the Northeast (NJ, NY, CT, MA, RI, NH,
  VT, ME) in Milestone 7.
- `With coords` = rows with `lat`/`lng` set. Only GA (55) and TN (30) carry
  coordinates; all other states are coordinate-free pending the reviewed
  geocoding pipeline (Milestones 1–4).
