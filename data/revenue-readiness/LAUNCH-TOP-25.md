## Recommended outreach order

1. **Tire + roadside, I-95 cluster (14 of the 25).** One corridor, one story:
   "drivers breaking down on I-95 are finding you here." Batch them so the
   pitch and follow-up are identical.
2. **Tire + roadside, I-40 / I-75 (6).**
3. **The 4 with no corridor** — still high-intent categories, weaker hook.
4. Then work down `OUTREACH-CANDIDATES.csv` by category priority.

Start with **claim** (free), not sponsorship. A claimed listing is a warm
relationship and costs the business nothing; placement is the second
conversation, once they have seen the listing is real.

## Email template — claim a listing

> **Subject:** Your listing on the Trucking Life truck stop directory
>
> Hi — I run Trucking Life with Shawn, a directory drivers use to find truck
> stops, repair, parking and scales along the interstates.
>
> **[BUSINESS]** is listed at **[DIRECTORY URL]**. I built the listing from
> public information, so I would rather you check it than me guess: hours,
> services, phone, and whether anything is wrong.
>
> If you want to correct or claim it, reply or use the "Claim this listing"
> button on the page. It is free, and nothing changes until I have reviewed it.
>
> — Shawn

*No price, no traffic number, no promise. States plainly that the listing was
built from public information and that review precedes any change.*

## Phone script

> "Hi, is the owner or manager around? … My name's Shawn, I run a truck stop
> directory that drivers use to find repair and parking along **[CORRIDOR]**.
> You're listed on it — I built the listing from public information and I'd
> rather have you check it than have me guess. Are your hours and services
> right? … I can send you the link so you can see it. It's free to claim, and
> nothing changes until I've reviewed it. If you ever want to be featured
> higher on the corridor page, that's a separate conversation and I'd want to
> show you real numbers first."

Rules for the call: never state a price, never promise placement, ranking,
traffic or leads, and never say the listing is verified.

## Suggested experiment metrics

Measure with the events the funnel now emits (`directory_claim_interest`,
`directory_featured_interest`, `directory_inquiry_start/submit/fail`) plus the
existing listing-view beacon:

| Question | Metric |
|---|---|
| Do businesses notice the CTA? | claim-interest clicks ÷ listing views |
| Does the form convert? | inquiry submits ÷ inquiry starts |
| Is the form breaking? | inquiry fails ÷ (submits + fails) |
| Which categories respond? | submits grouped by `category` |
| Which corridors respond? | submits grouped by `corridor` |
| Does outreach beat organic? | submits with a campaign attribution vs without |

**No baseline is stated on purpose.** There is no historical data for these
events — they ship in this PR. Set targets after two weeks of real numbers.
